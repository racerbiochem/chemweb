Ionic Naming Cheat Sheet

Essential rules and polyatomic ions list for fast study.

1. Binary Compounds

Name the cation (metal) first, then the anion with the ending changed to -ide (e.g., Sodium Chloride).

2. Transition Metals

Use Roman Numerals in parentheses for metals with variable oxidation states (e.g., Iron(III) Oxide for Fe²⁺ vs Fe³⁺).

3. Polyatomic Ions

Keep the specific name of polyatomic ions as-is (e.g., CaSO₄ = Calcium Sulfate, not sulfide).
